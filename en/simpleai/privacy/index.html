SimpleAI Privacy Policy

How SimpleAI stores your conversations, who it communicates with, and what happens when you install Ollama through the app.

✓

Stored on your device

Conversation history and settings are stored only on your device.

✓

Nothing goes to the developer

Your conversations, text, settings and usage are not sent to the developer. There is no analytics or advertising.

✓

Limited destinations

Your messages go only to the Ollama you connect to (by default, your own PC).

Last updated2026/11/27
DeveloperPlainDock Tools
AppSimpleAI

1. Overview

SimpleAI (the "App") is an app for chatting with an AI (Ollama) that runs on your PC, or on a server you specify. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

2. Information sent to the developer

The App does not send your conversations, the text you enter, your settings or your usage to the developer (PlainDock Tools). The App contains no usage analytics, no automatic crash reporting and no advertising.

3. Who the App communicates with

Your conversations are sent only to the Ollama server specified as the "connection target" in Settings. By default, this is your own PC (http://localhost:11434).

  • Your conversation is sent to this target and used to receive the AI's reply.
  • The target is limited to your own PC (localhost) or an IP address on your local network. If you change it to another computer, your conversations are sent to that computer, and its administrator's handling applies. Specify only targets you trust.
  • Model downloads ("Model setup" in Settings) are performed by the connected Ollama, from Ollama's distribution site, Hugging Face and similar sources. The App does not download models directly.

Apart from the target above, the following communication happens only when you take the action. None of it includes your conversations.

  • Installing Ollama through "Setup" when it is not found on your PC (the direct-download edition only, not the Microsoft Store edition): only if you consent on the confirmation screen, the App downloads Ollama's installer from Ollama's official site (ollama.com) and runs it, after verifying the publisher's signature. Without your consent, it downloads and runs nothing. The Microsoft Store edition neither downloads nor runs the installer; it only opens the official page in your browser and guides you.
  • Buttons such as "Help" and "Open": these open PlainDock Tools' site (plaindocktools.com) or similar pages in your default browser.

4. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device.

  • Conversation history (titles, messages, replies, timestamps)
  • Settings (connection target, selected model, generation settings, system prompt, etc.)
  • The last result of the purchase-status check (whether you have Pro)

You can delete conversation history with "Delete" in the App. When you uninstall the App, the stored information is normally removed (Microsoft Store edition).

5. Purchases

Purchases, cancellations and refunds of the Pro plan are handled by the Microsoft Store. Neither the App nor the developer receives your payment information (such as card numbers). The App receives from the Microsoft Store only your purchase status, that is, which plan you have purchased. Microsoft's handling of information follows the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

6. Disclosure to third parties

Because the developer does not collect your information, there is nothing to disclose to third parties.

7. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the in-app document, and revise the last-updated date.
