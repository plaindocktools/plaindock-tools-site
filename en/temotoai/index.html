TemotoAI for Windows

Chat with AI. Keep it on your PC.

Ask an AI running on your own PC to draft, summarize, rephrase or brainstorm. Your conversations are not sent to the developer's servers.

Requires Ollama, a separate free app. Setup in TemotoAI guides you from installation to your first chat.

Get started Microsoft Store (coming soon)

What you need

01

Windows 10 / 11

TemotoAI is a Windows app.

02

Ollama (free)

The software that runs the AI. It is installed separately, and TemotoAI guides you through it.

03

Disk space and internet

AI models are a few GB to over ten GB and are downloaded once, so you need an internet connection for that step.

What it does

01

Picks an AI that fits your PC

It checks your GPU memory and RAM, recommends one of three models (light, standard or high quality), and installs it with one button. You can switch freely.

02

Task templates

Emails, replies, apologies, daily reports, meeting minutes, summaries, proofreading, rewriting, translation, brainstorming and more. Pick one, fill in the blanks, and send.

03

Read your files

Attach text-based files (.txt, .md, .csv, .json, etc.) and ask about their contents. To read PDF, Word and Excel files, use document chat in the Pro edition.

04

Keep and search conversations

Conversations are saved on your PC and can be searched by title and text. You can rename, pin and delete them.

05

Ask about your documents (Pro feature)

Register PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) and text documents, as many as you like, and ask about them together. Only the parts relevant to your question are given to the AI, and the answer comes with its sources (document name, page, heading, or sheet and row). The contents of your documents and the search index made from them are stored only on your PC, and deleting a document deletes its index. You can register up to 100 documents, up to 30 MB each.

The AI is instructed to say "the documents do not say" when something is not written, but its answers can be wrong. Please check important points against the original in the sources shown. The first time you use it, a small model for reading documents (about 620 MB) is installed.

Getting started

Just follow the notices in the app.

  1. When you start the app and Ollama is missing or not running, a notice appears.
  2. Press "Setup". It checks and installs Ollama, then gets the recommended AI, in that order.
  3. When it finishes, you can start chatting right away.

If Ollama is only stopped, press "Start Ollama" in the notice.

About installing Ollama

The direct-download edition asks for your consent, then downloads Ollama's installer from the official site (ollama.com), verifies the publisher's signature, and runs it. Without your consent, it downloads and runs nothing.

The Microsoft Store edition only opens the official download page and guides you. The app never runs the installer itself.

Choosing a model

The app recommends one that fits your PC. You can switch later.

ModelSuited PC (guide)Good for
LightGemma 3 4BWorks with little GPU memory or no dedicated GPU (slower)Simple tasks. Weak at math and at instructions to rewrite text
StandardGemma 4 12B12 GB+ GPU memory, 16 GB+ RAMEveryday use, including more involved requests
High qualityGemma 4 26B24 GB+ GPU memory, 32 GB+ RAMBest quality. Can be slow or fail on PCs without enough memory

Behavior was checked on the developer's own PC (16 GB GPU memory, 32 GB RAM). Speed and behavior may differ on other PCs.

Before you use it

  • AI answers can contain mistakes. Please verify anything important.
  • The light model is weak at math and at instructions to rewrite text.
  • Speed and behavior depend on your PC.
  • The terms of use for AI models (such as Gemma) follow their respective distributors.

Privacy

Your conversations are stored on your PC.

Conversation history and settings are stored only on your PC. Your conversations are never sent to the developer's servers. The only place your messages go is the Ollama you connect to (by default, your own PC). The connection is limited to your own PC or your local network.
