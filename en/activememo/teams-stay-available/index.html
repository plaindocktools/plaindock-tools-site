Why does Teams switch to "Away"?

According to Microsoft, your status changes to Away when the PC has had no input for a few minutes or when the PC is locked, and to Offline when the PC goes to sleep (Microsoft Learn). Reading a document, watching a video, or waiting for a job all count, because none of them use the mouse or keyboard.

Main ways to see "Away" less often

  • Set a status message so people understand even when the status changes.
  • Put events on your calendar so the reason is visible.
  • Adjust the screen-off, sleep, and lock times within your organization’s policy.
  • Use software that keeps your PC active while you are at your desk (such as ActiveMemo).

On a work PC, installing software or automated input may be restricted. Check your organization’s rules first.

Keep your PC active at your desk with ActiveMemo

ActiveMemo is a Windows app that combines Keep Active, which keeps your PC active while idle, with quick local notes.

  1. Install ActiveMemo from Microsoft Store.
  2. Keep Active starts turned on. You can switch it on or off at any time with the switch at the bottom left.
  3. It keeps working when minimized. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space to minimize or restore the window.
  4. Write down whatever you think of while you are at your desk.

What ActiveMemo cannot do

Before you start

  • Keep Active does not work while your PC is asleep or the screen is locked.
  • Closing the app with the × button also stops Keep Active.
  • How long a status such as Teams stays green can vary with your setup and your organization's settings.
  • Please follow your employer's or organization's rules. We do not recommend using it to misrepresent your working status.

FAQ

Can I lock Teams on "Available" permanently?

We cannot promise that. ActiveMemo is an app that keeps your PC active while you are at your desk. It does not guarantee your Teams status.

Is it fine to walk away?

It does not work while the PC is asleep or the screen is locked. Follow your employer’s rules for when you leave your desk.

Is it free?

Every feature is free for 7 days from the first launch. To keep using it after that, you need ActiveMemo Pro (a subscription). Without Pro you can still view, copy, and export your existing notes.
