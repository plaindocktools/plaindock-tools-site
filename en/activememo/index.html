Redesigned in 3.0

Keep Active and quick local notes in one app.

ActiveMemo combines Keep Active, which keeps your PC active while idle, with a note list, editor, and search in one compact Windows app. Version 3.0 brings a clean window with your notes in a sidebar and an editor beside them, plus light and dark themes.

01

Keep Active

Keep your PC active and reduce interruptions caused by sleep or display timeout. It keeps going while the app is minimized, and you can turn it on or off at any time with the switch at the bottom left.

02

A clean notes window

Capture checklists, reference links, and progress notes with auto-save. Pin notes, reorder them by dragging, and drag and drop files or text to add notes.

Search, light and dark

Keep Teams active. Keep notes close.

Keep your PC active while idle and capture work notes right away. In the developer's setup, Teams stayed green for several hours without mouse or keyboard input.

Stay active, even minimized

Keep Active continues while the app is minimized. It starts on when you launch the app, and Ctrl + Alt + Space minimizes or restores the window.

Find saved notes fast

Filter notes by title or content in the sidebar. Press Ctrl + F to search within the open note. Your edits are saved automatically.

Light or dark. Your choice.

Choose Match system, Light, or Dark. Switch the display language between English and Japanese. The window position and size are restored the next time you open the app.

Notes stay on your PC

Your notes are stored locally on your PC, and no cloud account is required for notes. Export or import them as JSON files.

Before you start

  • Keep Active does not work while your PC is asleep or the screen is locked.
  • Closing the app with the × button also stops Keep Active.
  • How long Teams stays green can vary with your setup and your organization's settings.
  • Please follow your employer's or organization's rules.

Local-first notes

Your notes stay on your PC.

Note content is never sent to the developer's servers. Copy text or export your notes whenever you need to. The app-specific privacy policy explains what is stored.
