How to stop Teams showing "Away" at your desk: start with the easy things

Teams shows "Away" when there has been no input for a while, even if you are at your desk. Here are the options, in the order that is easiest to try, with cautions.

Why does it show "Away"?

According to Microsoft, your status changes to Away when the PC has had no input for a few minutes or when the PC is locked, and to Offline when the PC goes to sleep (Microsoft Learn). Reading a document, watching a video, or waiting for a job all count.

Microsoft’s community answers say the time before Teams shows Away cannot be changed in Teams settings (a 2020 answer; this may have changed with updates).

Option 1: Set a status message

Choose "Set status message" from your profile and write something like "I may look away while reading, but you can reach me." Even when the status changes, people are less likely to misread it.

Option 2: Put focus time on your calendar

Put your focus time on your calendar. It shows the reason to the people around you.

Option 3: Review screen-off, sleep, and lock times

If the PC turns off its screen or locks quickly even though you are at your desk, lengthen the times a little within your organization’s policy. On a work PC they may not be changeable.

Option 4: Use software that keeps your PC active while you are at your desk

ActiveMemo is one example. It is a Windows app that combines Keep Active, which keeps your PC active while idle, with quick local notes. It starts turned on and keeps working when minimized.

In the developer’s environment, the green Teams status stayed on for several hours without any mouse or keyboard input. Results can vary with your setup and your organization’s settings.

If you only want to keep the PC awake, Awake in Microsoft PowerToys may be enough.

What not to do

  • Using it to look present during hours when you are not working.
  • Using software or devices your employer prohibits.
  • Installing scripts or software from unknown sources.

What ActiveMemo cannot do

Before you start

  • Keep Active does not work while your PC is asleep or the screen is locked.
  • Closing the app with the × button also stops Keep Active.
  • How long a status such as Teams stays green can vary with your setup and your organization's settings.
  • Please follow your employer's or organization's rules. We do not recommend using it to misrepresent your working status.
